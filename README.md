# Insta Mart Sales Analysis Dashboard
 
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing  Insta Mart's grocery sales performance, covering **$1.20M** in total sales across **8,523 items**.
 
![Dashboard Preview](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/46303904-cfe8-40c2-8802-aedbd99e76b8)
 
## 📊 Overview
 
- **Total Sales:** $1.20M
- **No. of Items:** 8,523
- **Avg Sales:** 141
- **Avg Rating:** 3.9
## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas
 
- **Outlet Establishment Trend** — Sales growth from 2012 to 2022, peaking in 2018
- **Item Type Breakdown** — Performance across categories (Fruits & Vegetables, Snack Foods, Household, etc.)
- **Fat Content Distribution** — Low Fat vs. Regular item sales comparison
- **Outlet Size & Location** — Sales split by outlet size (Small/Medium/High) and location tier (Tier 1/2/3)
- **Outlet Type Performance** — Revenue, avg sales, and rating comparison across Grocery Store, Supermarket Type1/2/3
## 🛠️ Tools Used
 
- **Power BI** — Dashboard design & interactivity
- **DAX** — Custom measures and calculations
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning & transformation
## 💡 Key Insight
 
Supermarket Type1 drives ~66% of total revenue, while overall sales growth has plateaued since 2018 — highlighting a concentration risk and an opportunity to diversify investment toward high-performing Tier 3 outlets.
